PROMPT — ilustração do Capítulo 4 ("A Escolha")

Cinematic romantic illustration. Same art style, same warm golden palette and the same two
characters as the previous illustrations in this series: a woman in her late twenties with
long wavy honey-brown hair, and a tall man with dark curly hair and a short beard.

Early morning on the wooden veranda of a rustic beach house on a Brazilian tropical coast.
Warm golden sunlight, turquoise sea, palm trees and green hills in the background. Same
weathered wood, lace textiles, candle lanterns and potted flowers as the earlier images.

She stands at the wooden railing in a long white lace dress, seen in three-quarter view. In
one hand she holds a smartphone, its screen glowing with unread notifications. Her other
hand is open, palm up, and a thin white-gold ring rests on it. Delicate glowing golden rune
symbols trace softly along her forearm. Her expression is thoughtful and resolved — a woman
weighing two lives.

A few steps behind her he sits on a wicker chair, wearing an open white linen shirt, calm
and patient, watching her decide without interrupting. A small golden rune glows faintly on
his inner forearm.

On the low table beside him: an open leather-bound book with handwritten pages, a blue-and-
white ceramic cup, sprigs of dried lavender. Tiny golden light particles drift in the air.

Warm cinematic lighting, soft shallow depth of field, photorealistic, highly detailed.
Portrait orientation, aspect ratio 4:5, 1122x1402.

NOTAS

Composição escolhida de propósito: o capítulo é a manhã seguinte, mas pedir quarto, cama ou
casal seminu é o caminho mais curto para o filtro recusar. A varanda resolve melhor o
capítulo de qualquer forma — "A Escolha" é o celular numa mão e a aliança na outra, não a
cama. E ainda rima com a capa e com a `ilus-04`, que também são varanda.

Palavras evitadas de propósito: cama, quarto, lençol, sensual, íntimo, paixão, amantes,
desnudo, seminu, após a noite. O prompt não precisa de nenhuma delas.

Se o gerador ainda recusar, os dois ajustes mais prováveis de destravar, nesta ordem:
1. trocar `long white lace dress` por `long white linen sundress`
2. tirar `she stands at the railing` e pôr `she stands beside a wooden table`, afastando a
   leitura de varanda de quarto

Se ele aceitar mas a composição vier fraca, o detalhe que mais importa preservar é a
**assimetria das mãos**: celular aceso numa, aliança solta na outra. É o que faz a imagem
contar o capítulo.
